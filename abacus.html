From Abacus

Before we start learning computers, programming, and the main curriculum, let's ask a simple question: How did we get from this... to this? Computers did not appear suddenly. They are the result of hundreds of years of people trying to solve one basic problem: How can we make calculations and work easier and faster? An old calculator or abacus eventually led to the computers, smartphones, and AI systems we use today.
